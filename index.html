KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Important Consumer Rights

Awareness makes consumers more confident and helps them make better decisions.

🛡️

Right to Safety

Consumers should be protected from goods and services that may be dangerous to health or property.

ℹ️

Right to Information

Consumers should receive correct information about quality, quantity, price, ingredients and important terms.

⚖️

Right to Choose

Consumers should have access to a variety of products and services at fair and competitive prices.

📢

Right to be Heard

Consumer complaints and interests should receive proper consideration by businesses and authorities.

🔄

Right to Redressal

Consumers can seek a remedy when they face defective goods, deficient services or unfair practices.

🎓

Right to Consumer Education

Consumers should have access to knowledge that helps them make informed choices and protect themselves.

INDIAN CONTEXT

Consumer Protection in India

Consumer awareness is supported by laws, institutions and digital tools. The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 replaced the earlier 1986 law and strengthened the framework for protecting consumers.

1986India introduced the Consumer Protection Act, 1986 to provide a framework for consumer dispute redressal.
2019The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 was enacted as a modern replacement for the 1986 Act.
2020The 2019 Act came into force, bringing new provisions relevant to modern markets and consumers.

Why this matters today

Online shopping, digital payments, apps and social-media advertising have changed how consumers buy goods and services. Technology can make information and complaint processes more accessible, but consumers still need to verify information and keep records.

From Purchase to Complaint

Purchase

2

3

Contact

Use the seller's official support or complaint channel and clearly explain the issue.

4

Redressal

If the issue is not resolved, consumers can learn about appropriate formal redressal mechanisms.
